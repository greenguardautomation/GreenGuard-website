How it works

Every module finds and removes weeds on its own, while the tractor drives at 1–3 km/h.

  1. Find

    Cameras see every plant in the row under a light-sealed hood. The hood has its own light, so the result is the same in sun, cloud and dusk.

  2. Aim

    The computer in the module tells crop from weed and finds each weed's growth point, the point it regrows from.

  3. Remove

    The laser heats the growth point. The weed stops growing and dies over the following days.

Vision and AI

Telling a seedling weed from a seedling crop is the hard part. Ecoray does it on the machine, while you drive.

Sees every plant

Colour cameras see every plant in the row while the tractor drives.

Its own light

The hood has its own light, so the cameras see the same in sun, cloud and dusk.

No connection needed

The computer sits in the module, so Ecoray works without mobile coverage in the field.

Trained on Danish fields

About one million field images of crops, broad-leaved weeds and grasses.

Laser

A weed regrows from its growth point. Hit that point precisely and you don't need to burn the whole plant.

Two lasers per module

Each module has two lasers. Together they cover 60 cm of working width.

Protected in the module

The lasers sit sealed inside the module, away from dust and water.

Hits while you drive

Ecoray follows how the ground moves under the module, so each shot lands where the weed is, not where it was.

Built in a cleanroom

The optics are built and aligned in a cleanroom at Aarhus University, through ESA BIC Denmark.

Safety by design

The AI decides what to shoot. It never decides whether the laser is allowed to fire.

Sealed hood

The laser only fires inside a closed hood. A rubber skirt follows the ground and keeps the light in.

Hardwired interlocks

The safety functions are wired in hardware and work independently of the software.

Standards

Designed to EN ISO 12100, EN ISO 11553-1 and EN 60825-1, with CE marking under the EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230.

Built for the field

IP69K enclosure

Sealed aluminium enclosure that stands up to rain, dust and high-pressure washing.

Liquid cooling

A closed coolant loop keeps the laser at its operating temperature through a long day in the field.

Steady aim

The optics are isolated from the tractor's vibration, so the aim point stays put.
