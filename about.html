We are GreenGuard

An engineering company in Aarhus building laser weeding for European farms.

Meet the team Contact us

Why we exist

Weeds in the crop row are still removed by hand or with herbicides. Labour is scarce and expensive, pesticide rules keep tightening, and growers need a way to keep fields clean without chemicals.

GreenGuard was founded in 2024 to make chemical-free weeding practical and affordable for small and mid-sized farms, not just the largest operations. We design the optics, electronics, software and machine ourselves, and test them in Danish fields every season.
