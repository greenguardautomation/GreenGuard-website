Try Ecoray free on your own farm

For farms in Denmark: the public SMV:Robot scheme can fund a trial of Ecoray in your own rows for up to three months. GreenGuard is an approved robot integrator under SMV:Digital, so we help with the application, set Ecoray up on your tractor and train you to use it.

How it works

  1. Sign up

    Fill in the form below. It takes two minutes and commits you to nothing.

  2. We apply together

    We call you, check whether your farm can apply, and help with the application when the scheme opens. Application rounds are short, often just a week, so it pays to be ready.

  3. Ecoray runs on your farm

    If the grant is approved, we set Ecoray up on your tractor and train you and your team to use it. We plan the trial to fall in your weeding season as far as possible.

Can my farm apply?

SMV:Robot is part of the Danish SMV:Digital programme and is open to farms. In the latest round the business had to, among other things:

  • have a Danish CVR number and be an SME under the EU definition
  • have at least 3 full-time equivalents and at least DKK 2 million in revenue in the last financial year
  • be eligible for de minimis aid

We go through the requirements with you before applying. Grants are awarded by SMV:Digital, not by GreenGuard. See the current terms (in Danish) at Virksomhedsguiden.dk.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

The grant covers up to DKK 125,000 excl. VAT of the trial: loan of the machine, setup, service, training and removal. The scheme does not require any co-financing. The grant is paid after the trial, so the farm pays the costs up front in the meantime.

When can we apply?

SMV:Digital opens the scheme in short rounds, typically one week at a time. The latest round closed on 2 November 2026. Sign up and we'll contact you when the next round opens.

What if we don't get the grant?

Then it has cost you nothing. We can show Ecoray on your farm instead, or quote you for renting it in the 2027 season.

Do I have to buy Ecoray afterwards?

No. When the trial ends you can buy or rent Ecoray, or hand it back.

Which crops can it weed?

Ecoray is built for carrots, onions, beets and Nordmann fir. If you grow something else, write it in the form and we'll tell you how well it fits.
